# 서핑 (Surfing)

User: "서퍼는 파도위에 보드로 서있는 모습이야. 파도를 온전히 타고 있고 파도가 쭉 몰려오고 그것 위에 타고 있을 수 있어야해. 서퍼는 파도를 온전히 느낄 수 있도록" (The surfer stands on a board on the wave, fully riding it; the wave keeps rolling in and the surfer rides on top of it; the surfer fully feels the wave.)
Notes: a big wave face with a curling, peeling lip; the surfer stands on the board riding along the face — crouched stance, knees bent, arms out for balance, body leaning into the wave; a board with a shaped pointed nose, tail and rocker; spray or a wake trailing from the rail. The surfer feels the wave: the body responds to it (rises and drops along the face, legs compress and extend, arms counter-balance). Loop: the wave keeps rolling in while the surfer carves up and down the face; spray pulses. The figure must be a crafted silhouette (see Figures in STYLE.md) — absolutely no ball head or stick limbs.
Frames: 32-48.
