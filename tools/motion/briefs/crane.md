# 학 (Crane) — id: crane

User: "북두칠성 안보이니까 빼고 대신 학으로 바꿔줘." (Replace the Big Dipper with a crane.)

Notes: the red-crowned crane (두루미) of Korean painting — long legs, long neck, elegant. Pick the pose that reads best at 18 pt (e.g., standing on one leg, or slow wing beats in flight, or a dance with wings raised). Motion should be felt but calm and noble: a slow wingbeat, a neck turn, a bow, or the dance. Must read as a crane (not a heron/stork/flamingo blob) at real size — the long neck and legs and the wing silhouette are the cues. Monochrome only (no red crown colour; a small cut or front shape can suggest the crown).
Frames: 48–64.

## Update (user, after seeing a flying crane that read as a goose)
"학은 날아가는 것보다 서 있는게 더 아름다워." → The crane STANDS. Flying is out.
What made the flying version fail: plump body + short neck = goose/duck. A crane needs: a very slender, long S-curved neck, a small head with a long straight beak, long thin legs (often one leg tucked), a slim tapering body with the bushy "tail" (actually tertial plumes) drooping at the back. Elegance comes from thin long lines against one compact body mass — like a Korean ink painting of 두루미.
