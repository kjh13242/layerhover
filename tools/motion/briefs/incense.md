# 향 (Incense)

User: "인센스는 받침 얇게 하고 인센스 끝에 동그란 거 없이 그냥 막대로 하고 끝에서 타고있는 연기가 모락모락 올라오면 돼. 그 연기의 모습이 하나이기도 두개이기도 하며 변화를 만들어. 연기가 올라오는 모습도 달라져야겠어. 보통은 한개 연기, 가끔 두개 연기." (Thin holder; no round thing at the end of the stick, just a stick; smoke curls up from the burning end. The smoke is sometimes one plume, sometimes two, and it keeps changing; the way it rises changes too. Usually one plume, occasionally two.)
Notes: a slim, elegant incense holder (thin — e.g., a long low tray or a small boat-shaped holder) and a single slim stick resting at an angle; NO round dot at the tip (the burning end may be suggested by a tiny gap or a slightly thinner ash segment). Smoke: calligraphic tapered ribbons rising and curling (wispy S-curves that thin to nothing), the upper part in fade(). Usually one plume; sometimes it divides into two for a while, then merges back. The shape of the rising smoke must keep changing (not one repeating sine). Holder on y = 16.
Frames: 48-64.

## Update (latest user direction — overrides the notes above)
- Composition: the universal incense holder — a thin rectangular plank on the ground, a long thin stick inserted diagonally near one end (not a stick lying on a dish: that read as a cigarette).
- User: "두줄 연기 꼭 유지해야할까? 연기만 잘 표현되면 될거 같아. 타고 있으며 위로 올라가며 흔들리는 연기들. 그걸 잘 표현하기 위해 연기 폭의 변경이나 여러개 연기 표현은 필요해. 필요에 따라 선택하는게 좋아. 약간 달라도 잘 표현되고 잘 보이기만 하면 돼."
  → Two plumes are no longer required. What matters: smoke that is clearly burning, rising and wavering, and clearly visible at actual size. Width changes or several wisps are tools to use as needed.
