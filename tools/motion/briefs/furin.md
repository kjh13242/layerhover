# 풍경 (Wind chime, Japanese furin) — id: furin

User: "풍경으로 하자. 바람에 흔들리는 풍경. 일본 스타일 풍경으로 위에 종모양 유리 같은 구조체 있고 아래 종이 길다란거 달려서 바람에 따라 흔들흔들 하는거로."
(A wind chime swaying in the wind. Japanese-style furin: a bell-shaped glass dome on top, a long paper strip hanging below, swinging back and forth in the wind.)

Notes: hung from a short cord at the top. Glass bell = a dome (could read as glass via a cut highlight or a thin rim, not a solid blob). Inside: the clapper (zetsu) on a string, and below it the long paper strip (tanzaku) that catches the wind — the strip is the main motion carrier: it swings, twists slightly, flutters at its end. The bell itself swings much less (pendulum from the top). Occasionally a gust makes it swing wider and the clapper touches the rim (you may suggest the "ring" with a tiny visual cue). Calm, summer, cool. Must read as a wind chime at 18 pt, not a lamp or a jellyfish.
Frames: 48–64.
