# 목련 (Magnolia)

User: "목련은 펴서 바람에 흔들리는 모습으로 하자. 가만히 있다가 일정 시간되면 흔들흔들" (A magnolia in bloom swaying in the wind; it stays still, then after a while it sways.)
Notes: a Korean white magnolia bloom (백목련): a goblet or tulip-shaped flower of thick, spoon-shaped tepals — the outer ones opening slightly, the inner ones cupped — on a bare woody twig (zig-zag twig, perhaps one furry bud). Tepals separated by negative space (cutAround + front for overlaps). Motion: still for about half of the loop, then a breeze: the branch sways about the twig base, the flower lags behind (follow-through), tepals flutter slightly, then it settles back to stillness. Clear sway (at least 2 pt at the flower).
