# 북두칠성 (Big Dipper)

User: "물위에 보름달은 별로다. 북두칠성으로 하고 시간에 따라 그 별 하나가 빛났다가 줄어드는 변화를 주는걸로. 몇 개별이 시간에 따라 순차적인 변화야." (Replace the moon with the Big Dipper; over time one star at a time shines and then shrinks back; several stars change in sequence.)
Notes: the seven stars in their true arrangement. Reference positions in pt, already fitted to the live area (you may scale, shift or rotate slightly for composition but keep the shape): Alkaid (2.5, 11.3), Mizar (4.3, 8.3), Alioth (6.7, 7.7), Megrez (9.7, 7.1), Phecda (11.4, 8.9), Merak (15.5, 7.5), Dubhe (15.4, 4.5). The handle curves down to the left; the bowl opens upward between Megrez and Dubhe. Brightness for sizing: Alioth 1.8, Dubhe 1.8, Alkaid 1.9, Mizar 2.0 (with faint Alcor right next to it — optional detail), Merak 2.4, Phecda 2.4, Megrez 3.3 (smallest). Stars are crafted sparkles (concave 4-point, optionally short diagonal rays), sized by brightness; the asterism links are fine hairlines that stop short of each star (or dotted). Motion: one star at a time brightens — grows into a larger sparkle with longer rays (a glint), then shrinks back — sequentially through the stars, with quiet moments in between. Each brightening must be clearly visible at actual size (e.g., a star growing from about 1.6 pt to about 4 pt across).
