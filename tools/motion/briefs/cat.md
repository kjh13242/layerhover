# 고양이와 나비 (Cat and butterfly)

User: "고양이는 바닥에 철푸덕 누워서 발 위로 올리고 나비 잡으려고 발길질 하는 모습이면 좋겠어. 평화로운 느낌이 들 수 있게" (The cat is flopped on the floor, paws raised, pawing at a butterfly it wants to catch. It should feel peaceful.)
Notes: a cat flopped lazily on the floor (on its back or side, belly up — relaxed, sprawling), head resting on the floor with ears visible (closed eyes as a cut are a nice touch), front paws raised toward a butterfly fluttering above; the cat lazily bats at it (slow swipes, not frantic); the tail curls and flicks lazily. Peaceful and playful. Crafted cat anatomy: a rounded head with distinct pointed ears and a muzzle bump, a soft chubby body curve, paws with rounded mitten tips, a long tapered tail. Butterfly: proper wings (forewing and hindwing) that flap (the wing shape narrows and widens), a body between, a fluttering path. Floor at y = 16 (a floor line is optional).
