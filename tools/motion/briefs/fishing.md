# 앉아서 낚시 (Fishing)

User: "낚시는 가만히 앉아있지만 낚시대는 짧게 위아래로 입질이 오고 어느 정도 되면 낚아서 낚시 꾼이 확 채나가는 모습이어야 해. 그러고 다시 낚시대를 드리우고 입질 상황이 반복되게. 입질 2번 후 낚아채고 다시 반복" (The angler sits still; the rod gets short up-and-down nibbles; after a while the angler strikes and yanks the fish out. Then the rod is cast again and the nibbling repeats. Two nibbles, then the strike, then repeat.)
Notes: an angler sitting calmly (on a dock edge, a rock or a low stool by the water), with a hat (straw or bucket hat gives character), both hands on the rod. Rod = a tapered ribbon (thick grip to fine tip) that BENDS under load (use bend()). Line = hairline. Float/bobber crafted (two-tone via a cut). One loop (about 8 s): idle -> nibble 1 (rod tip dips in 2-3 short quick bobs, the float dips, small ripple) -> idle -> nibble 2 -> STRIKE (the angler leans back sharply, the rod whips up and bends, the line goes taut, a fish with a proper fish silhouette — body and tail fin — is pulled out of the water with a little splash) -> the fish swings and flaps -> recast (rod swings forward, the float lands with a ripple) -> idle. The angler is still except during the strike and recast. Water surface at the bottom.
Frames: 56-64.
